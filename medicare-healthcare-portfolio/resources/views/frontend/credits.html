Image credits

Every photograph in this portfolio demonstration is stock material published on Pexels. Each file is stored locally in public/assets/images/, resized and cropped to the aspect ratio the layout uses.

Stock photography, not real patients or facilities

The people, clinics, laboratories, products and suppliers shown are generic stock photographs. None of them depicts a person, business or product described in the fictional datasets, and none of them is presented as a real MediCare partner.

Licence

All photographs are used under the Pexels License, which permits free use — including commercial use and modification — without requiring attribution. Attribution is provided on this page as good practice and to make the origin of every file traceable.

The following assets are original vector artwork created for this project and are not covered by the table below: the MediCare logo (public/assets/images/logo.svg), the favicon (public/assets/icons/favicon.svg), all interface icons, and the CSS-only decorative elements.
