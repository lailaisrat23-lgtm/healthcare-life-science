These terms illustrate the sections a healthcare platform would typically publish. They do not create any binding agreement.

1. Acceptance of these terms

By browsing this demonstration you acknowledge that it is a portfolio project presenting fictional content, and that it is not a healthcare provider, pharmacy, laboratory, manufacturer or supplier.

2. Website usage

You may browse, search, filter and interact with the demonstration freely. You agree not to present the demonstration content as real healthcare information, and not to enter real personal or medical information into any form.

3. Illustrative service information

All doctors, clinics, laboratories, tests, prices, products, manufacturers, suppliers, appointments, reports, statistics and availability windows are fictional sample data. No price, turnaround time, fee or service description is a real offer.

4. Demo interactions

Forms, cart previews, searches, filters and status lookups behave locally in your browser. No appointment is booked, no order is placed, no prescription is issued, no payment is taken and no message is delivered. Previously displayed demo references are not retrievable later.

5. Absence of real healthcare transactions

This site cannot provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, triage, prescriptions or emergency assistance. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for real health concerns, and contact local emergency services in an emergency.

The demonstration is provided without warranty of any kind. The project authors are not liable for any loss arising from reliance on fictional demonstration content, from the absence of real clinical functionality, or from the availability of the static files.

8. External links

The project avoids runtime calls to external services. Where an external resource is referenced in documentation, it is provided for reference only and is not controlled by this project.

9. Accessibility statement

Accessibility was treated as a baseline requirement: semantic HTML, keyboard operation, visible focus indicators, live-region status messages, sufficient contrast and reduced-motion support. Automated checks cannot demonstrate full conformance, so known limitations are documented alongside the project.

These sample terms may be revised as the demonstration develops. A production version would record an effective date and revision history.
