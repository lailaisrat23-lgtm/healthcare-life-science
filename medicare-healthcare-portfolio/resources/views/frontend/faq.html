Frequently asked questions

Answers grouped by area: appointments, telemedicine, clinics and doctors, pharmacy, diagnostics, pharmaceutical suppliers, and privacy and security. Every answer explains how this static demonstration behaves.

Appointments

No. The form performs client-side validation only and displays confirmation-style demo feedback. Nothing is transmitted, stored or sent to any clinic, doctor or scheduling system.

You can look up the demonstration appointment IDs shown on the appointment tracking page (for example MCA-2401-0001). These are fixed sample records used to illustrate how a status timeline could look.

Telemedicine

No. The telemedicine pages explain how a remote consultation workflow could work. No video calling, messaging, triage or real-time communication is implemented.

The demonstration lists general considerations such as a stable connection, a quiet space and a device with a working camera and microphone. This is general information, not medical advice.

Clinics and doctors

No. Every doctor profile, qualification line, clinic and address is fictional demonstration content created for this portfolio. Nothing implies licensure, registration or availability in the real world.

Search and filters run entirely in the browser against a local JavaScript dataset. There is no server, database or external service involved.

Pharmacy

No. The pharmacy section is a static catalogue demonstration. The cart preview uses local browser state and cannot initiate payment, ordering or prescription handling.

The label demonstrates how a real storefront might distinguish restricted categories in the interface. It is applied to fictional sample products only.

Diagnostics
